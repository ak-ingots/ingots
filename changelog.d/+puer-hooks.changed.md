The PUER flow is final and its hooks start with an underscore: `runner.run()` and the `slots_` methods are `@typing.final`. `Project.configure()` and `Project.shut_down()` are gone: the runner awaits the `_configure()` hook and shuts the project down through the final, internal `_do_shut_down()`. A subclass overrides `Project._configure()` and `Project._shut_down()` (no `super()` needed for them), `BaseRunner._run_workflow()`, `_stop_workflow()`, `_create_project()` and the new `_prepare_run()`, `BaseCommand._run()` and `_stop()` and `CliNode._cli_prepare_parser()` and `_cli_build_args()`. `check_slots()` is internal now. Ingots checks its overrides with mypy's `explicit-override`, and the docs mark them with `@typing.override`.
